\section{Professional Summary}
Computational fluid dynamics researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology with industrial
experience in conservation-based transient modelling, heat transfer, and coupled 0-D/3-D simulation.
Develop Python models and numerical workflows to analyse pressure, temperature, and flow response;
compare predictions with measurements and numerical reference solutions. Research spans compressible
CFD, reduced combustion modelling, non-isothermal flows, and fluid--structure interaction (FSI).
Seeking to contribute model development, scientific programming, and verification and validation
skills to Blykalla's fluid-system analysis.

\section{Technical Skills}
\textbf{Fluid and thermal modelling:} Mass, momentum, and energy conservation; 0-D transient models;
compressible flow; combustion and heat transfer; nozzle flow; non-isothermal low-Mach-number flows;
natural convection.\\[3pt]
\textbf{Model development and coupling:} Python-based physical models; coupled 0-D/3-D simulations;
transient boundary conditions; finite-volume and finite-element methods; compressible LES;
strong two-way CFD--FEM coupling.\\[3pt]
\textbf{Verification and validation:} Comparison with measured pressure histories; parameter-sensitivity
studies; mesh and time-step convergence; analytical/FEM cross-checks; reduced/detailed kinetic-model
comparisons; assessment of model assumptions and limitations.\\[3pt]
\textbf{Programming and scientific computing:} Python, MATLAB, C/C++, Fortran, Mathematica;
scientific data analysis and visualisation; HPC simulation workflows using NAISS resources at PDC and NSC.\\[3pt]
\textbf{Simulation software:} OpenFOAM, Simcenter STAR-CCM+, COMSOL Multiphysics, ABAQUS,
ANSYS; Tecplot, Gnuplot, Inkscape, LaTeX.\\[3pt]
\textbf{Additional analysis:} Acoustic wave and resonance modelling; structural eigenmodes;
power and cross-spectra; coherence and phase; spectral proper orthogonal decomposition (SPOD);
hyperelasticity and flow-induced vibration.
